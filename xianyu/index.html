05 — FAQ

Quick answers常见问题

Can I bring kids? · 可以带小孩吗？

No — Xianyu is strictly 18+ at all hours, so kids can’t come in.不可以，本店全程严格 18+，不接待小孩。

Can I smoke or vape? · 可以抽烟或电子烟吗？

E-cigarettes and vapes are fine; cigarette smoking is not allowed inside.电子烟 / vape 可以，场内不可抽烟。

Is there a minimum spend? · 有最低消费吗？

Only sofas, and only on Fri, Sat and PH-eve nights: RM500 for a sofa (up to 7 guests) or RM1,000 for a big sofa (up to 12). On weekdays and the PH day itself there is no sofa minimum.只有沙发有最低消费，且仅限周五、周六和公假前夕：普通沙发 RM500（最多 7 位），大沙发 RM1,000（最多 12 位）。平日和公假当天没有沙发最低消费。

Do I need to book? Is there a deposit? · 需要订位吗？要订金吗？

Walk-ins are welcome any day; on Fri, Sat and PH eves it’s worth a WhatsApp ahead. No deposit — we hold your table for 15 minutes past your booking time. Confirmations come in English, and you can chat with us in English or Chinese.每天都欢迎 walk-in；周五、周六和公假前夕建议先 WhatsApp 订位。无需订金，座位保留约 15 分钟。确认信息为英文，聊天用中文或英文都可以。

What are the hours, and when does the band play? · 几点营业？现场几点开始？

We’re open 6:30pm–2:30am daily, and live music starts at 9:30pm. The full week’s lineup is posted on Instagram every Monday.每天 6:30pm–2:30am 营业，现场 9:30pm 开始。每周一在 Instagram 公布整周演出安排。
