nana 研酒所 is a Japanese social live music bar at 2 Jalan SS 13/3A, SS13, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. Open daily 5pm–2am; live music starts around 9pm. Book on WhatsApp at +60 12-897 4221. Under-18 guests must leave by 8:30pm; strictly 18+ after.

Xianyu 咸鱼酒馆 is a live music bar at Unit A-G-03, Union Tower, Jalan Desa Bakti, Taman Desa, 58000 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Open daily 6:30pm–2:30am; live music starts around 9:30pm. Book on WhatsApp at +60 12-652 2885. Strictly 18+ at all hours.
