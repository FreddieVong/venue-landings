05 — FAQ

Quick answers常见问题

Can I bring someone under 18? · 可以带未满 18 岁的朋友吗？

Yes, in the early evening — under-18 guests must leave by 8:30pm, and the venue is strictly 18+ after that.可以在傍晚时段来，未满 18 岁须在 8:30pm 前离开，之后严格 18+。

Can I smoke or vape? · 可以抽烟或电子烟吗？

E-cigarettes and vapes are fine; cigarette smoking is not allowed inside.电子烟 / vape 可以，场内不可抽烟。

Is there a minimum spend? · 有最低消费吗？

Only the stage sofa carries a RM300 minimum, and only on Fri, Sat and PH-eve nights. On weekdays and the PH day itself there is no sofa minimum.只有舞台沙发有 RM300 最低消费，且仅限周五、周六和公假前夕。平日和公假当天没有沙发最低消费。

Do I need to book? Is there a deposit? · 需要订位吗？要订金吗？

Walk-ins are welcome any day; on Fri, Sat and PH eves it’s worth a WhatsApp ahead. No deposit — we hold your table for about 15 minutes past your booking time. Confirmations come in English, and you can chat with us in English or Chinese.每天都欢迎 walk-in；周五、周六和公假前夕建议先 WhatsApp 订位。无需订金，座位保留约 15 分钟。确认信息为英文，聊天用中文或英文都可以。

What are the hours, and when does the band play? · 几点营业？现场几点开始？

We’re open 5pm–2am daily, and live music usually starts around 9pm. The full week’s lineup is posted on Instagram every Monday.每天 5pm–2am 营业，现场大约 9pm 开始。每周一在 Instagram 公布整周演出安排。

Do you serve food? · 有吃的吗？

Yes — dinner first, music later. 800g pots of fresh clams (lala) from RM50, crispy hand-rolls from RM12, hand-wrap rice, pasta, chops, bar snacks and 3-inch cakes. Late-night ramen runs 9:30pm–12:30am. Full menu.有的，先吃饭再听歌：800g 啦啦锅 RM50 起、脆皮手卷 RM12 起、意面、扒类、小吃和 3 寸蛋糕；深夜拉面 9:30pm–12:30am。

Is there a happy hour? · 有欢乐时光吗？

Yes, before 8:30pm: beer buckets (5 bottles) are buy 1 free 1, 10 glasses of Guinness are RM175, crispy hand-rolls start at RM6.80, and the pork chop, chargrilled chicken and chicken chop are RM16.80.8:30pm 前：啤酒桶（5 瓶）买一送一，Guinness 10 杯 RM175，手卷 RM6.80 起，扒类 RM16.80。

Can I keep an unfinished bottle? · 酒可以寄存吗？

Yes. Spirits bottles are kept for 60 days and unfinished beer for 30 days.洋酒可寄存 60 天，啤酒可寄存 30 天。

Is nana halal? · 是清真吗？

No. nana is a bar, and the menu includes pork and alcohol.非清真：菜单有猪肉和酒精饮料。

Do you have non-alcoholic drinks? · 有无酒精饮料吗？

Yes. Most of the signature cocktails come in an alcohol-free version, from RM25.大部分招牌鸡尾酒都有无酒精版本，RM25 起。

Can I celebrate a birthday here? · 可以来庆生吗？

Yes. In your birthday month the Flaming Lamborghini is RM68 instead of RM128, and 3-inch cakes are RM28–RM29. WhatsApp ahead to hold a table.生日月火焰兰博基尼只要 RM68（原价 RM128），3 寸蛋糕 RM28–29。建议先 WhatsApp 订位。
